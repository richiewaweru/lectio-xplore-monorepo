Capability: choice.

Author a complete activity envelope: student-facing prompt, choice config, and feedback.

- Write the prompt as a clear question the learner reads directly. The planning brief is context only — do not copy it verbatim.
- Provide feedback.correct and feedback.incorrect messages tied to the question and distractors.
- Put options and the correct option id in config.

- Use at least two options with unique stable ids.
- Exactly one option is correct.
- Distractors must be plausible and tied to a stateable misconception.
- Do not use this for questions with several defensible answers.
