LECTIO V2 DOCUMENT PALETTE
Use pedagogical intent first. Then choose the simplest valid paper object.

OBJECTS
heading — structural title; bound to first following block; no box.
prose — explanatory paragraphs; free fragmentation; main column.
list — ordered/unordered/steps/glossary; split between items.
table — comparison/reference/timeline; repeating header; no enclosing box.
figure — image/SVG plus caption; atomic; main or spanning.
aside — short set-apart note; atomic; only boxed/bordered object; margin preferred.
worked-example — problem, reasoning steps, answer; split between steps.
questions — open response with marks and answer space; split between questions.
choices — lettered selected response; no radio controls.
answer-key — teacher checking/marking guidance.

INTENTS

orient [prose, figure, choices]
  label: Orient
  role: Open attention and establish a reason to learn
  cognitive_job: activate curiosity and situational framing
  guidance: Begin with a concrete phenomenon, dilemma, observation, or question. Do not explain the full idea yet.

activate-prior-knowledge [prose, list, questions, choices]
  label: Recall what you know
  role: Surface prerequisite knowledge
  cognitive_job: retrieve relevant prior knowledge
  guidance: Prompt recall of concepts required for this lesson. Keep it brief and diagnostic.

state-goal [prose, list]
  label: Learning goal
  role: Clarify the expected learning outcome
  cognitive_job: metacognitive goal setting
  guidance: State what the learner should be able to explain, do, compare, or justify.

define [prose, table, aside]
  label: Define
  role: Establish precise meaning
  cognitive_job: concept formation and vocabulary encoding
  guidance: Give a precise learner-appropriate definition, distinguish it from nearby terms, and include notation only when useful.

name-parts [list, table, figure]
  label: Name the parts
  role: Identify constituent elements
  cognitive_job: decomposition and labeling
  guidance: Identify the important parts of a system or idea and state each part's role.

classify [list, table, questions, choices]
  label: Classify
  role: Organize examples into categories
  cognitive_job: categorical reasoning
  guidance: Provide or elicit a rule for category membership and contrast borderline cases.

compare [table, prose, questions]
  label: Compare
  role: Expose similarities and differences
  cognitive_job: relational comparison
  guidance: Compare using consistent criteria. End with the distinction that matters most.

sequence [list, table, figure, questions]
  label: Put in order
  role: Represent an ordered process
  cognitive_job: temporal and procedural ordering
  guidance: Express steps or events in a meaningful order. Include dependencies and stopping conditions where relevant.

explain [prose, figure, table]
  label: Explain
  role: Build a coherent mental model
  cognitive_job: causal and conceptual understanding
  guidance: Explain why or how, not merely what. Use explicit relationships and avoid decorative segmentation.

explain-cause [prose, figure, table, questions]
  label: Explain cause and effect
  role: Connect causes, mechanisms, and outcomes
  cognitive_job: causal reasoning
  guidance: Identify cause, mechanism, and result. Separate correlation from mechanism when relevant.

trace-flow [figure, list, table, prose]
  label: Trace the flow
  role: Follow matter, energy, information, or value
  cognitive_job: systems tracing
  guidance: Track what enters, changes, moves, and leaves. Preserve identity and direction.

show-structure [figure, table, list]
  label: Show the structure
  role: Reveal spatial or hierarchical organization
  cognitive_job: structural visualization
  guidance: Show levels, containment, arrangement, or relationships that are hard to infer from prose.

demonstrate [worked-example, figure]
  label: Worked demonstration
  role: Model a complete method
  cognitive_job: procedural modeling
  guidance: Show a representative problem or task from setup through reasoning to answer.

model-thinking [worked-example, prose]
  label: Think aloud
  role: Expose expert decision making
  cognitive_job: metacognitive strategy modeling
  guidance: State why each decision is made, what alternatives were rejected, and what signal triggered the next step.

derive [worked-example, prose, table]
  label: Derive
  role: Build a result from prior statements
  cognitive_job: deductive reasoning
  guidance: Show each justified transformation. Do not skip the conceptual reason for a step.

interpret [prose, figure, table, questions]
  label: Interpret
  role: Translate representation into meaning
  cognitive_job: representational fluency
  guidance: Explain what a graph, formula, diagram, table, or result means in context.

apply [questions, worked-example, table]
  label: Apply
  role: Use knowledge in a familiar situation
  cognitive_job: near transfer
  guidance: Require selection and use of the learned idea in a recognizable context.

transfer [questions, worked-example]
  label: Transfer
  role: Use knowledge in a changed context
  cognitive_job: farther transfer and abstraction
  guidance: Change surface features enough that learners must recognize the underlying structure.

practise-guided [questions, worked-example]
  label: Guided practice
  role: Rehearse with structured support
  cognitive_job: scaffolded retrieval and execution
  guidance: Provide prompts, hints, partial steps, or gradually withdrawn support.

practise-independent [questions, choices]
  label: Independent practice
  role: Rehearse without scaffolding
  cognitive_job: independent retrieval and execution
  guidance: Require learners to produce or select answers without embedded hints.

check-understanding [questions, choices]
  label: Quick check
  role: Detect immediate misunderstanding
  cognitive_job: formative assessment
  guidance: Use a small number of high-signal items tied directly to the lesson objective.

diagnose-misconception [choices, questions, aside]
  label: Diagnose misconception
  role: Distinguish a known wrong model from the target model
  cognitive_job: misconception diagnosis
  guidance: Include distractors or prompts based on plausible misconceptions and require explanation where useful.

warn [aside, prose]
  label: Watch out
  role: Prevent a predictable error
  cognitive_job: error avoidance
  guidance: Name the error, why it seems plausible, and the correction. Keep it short.

emphasise [aside, prose]
  label: Key idea
  role: Mark a high-value statement
  cognitive_job: selective attention and encoding
  guidance: Set apart only the single statement learners should retain. Do not create multiple competing key ideas.

memory-aid [aside, list]
  label: Memory aid
  role: Support recall with a compact cue
  cognitive_job: mnemonic encoding
  guidance: Provide a short, accurate cue that does not replace understanding.

summarise [list, prose, table]
  label: Summarise
  role: Compress the lesson into essential relationships
  cognitive_job: schema consolidation
  guidance: Restate the minimum complete model, not a list of disconnected facts.

connect-forward [prose, list]
  label: What comes next
  role: Bridge current learning to the next idea
  cognitive_job: curricular coherence
  guidance: State what the current idea enables next and the unresolved question that follows.

connect-back [prose, list, questions]
  label: Use what came before
  role: Make prerequisite dependence explicit
  cognitive_job: knowledge integration
  guidance: Show which prior idea is being reused and exactly how it supports the current task.

reflect [questions, prose]
  label: Reflect
  role: Prompt learners to examine their understanding
  cognitive_job: metacognitive monitoring
  guidance: Ask learners to explain what changed in their thinking, what remains difficult, or which strategy helped.

investigate [questions, list, table, figure]
  label: Investigate
  role: Structure an inquiry or practical
  cognitive_job: scientific and empirical reasoning
  guidance: Specify question, variables, observations, and evidence expectations. Avoid pretending a screen simulation is a paper practical.

evaluate [questions, table, prose]
  label: Evaluate
  role: Judge a claim, method, or evidence
  cognitive_job: critical evaluation
  guidance: Require criteria, evidence, a judgment, and limitations.

answer-key [answer-key]
  label: Teacher guidance
  role: Provide checking and marking guidance
  cognitive_job: assessment interpretation
  guidance: Supply correct answers, acceptable alternatives, working, misconceptions, and marks where appropriate.

PLANNING RULES
- Output ordered moves with explicit position.
- Repeated object types are valid.
- No more than two asides in a normal lesson unless justified.
- Never choose an object because it looks attractive.
- Do not use object names as student-visible labels.
- Do not plan screen interactions.
- Support/core/extension differences must alter structure or scaffolding.
- Figure plans must state what the figure must show and why prose is insufficient.
- Every required lesson objective must map to at least one move.
