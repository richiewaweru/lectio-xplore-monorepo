Table of Contents
1. Make the learner feel the need for a method by facing a total they cannot get from one memorised fact, and fix the concrete case of 3 boxes of 24 cookies.
2. Build the procedure: split the two-digit factor into tens and ones, multiply each part by the one-digit factor, and name the two partial products.
3. Let the learner see the boundary of the method by contrasting it with two similar-looking shortcuts that fail because they lose the tens value.
4. Check whether the learner can decompose a two-digit factor into tens and ones and state the two partial products for the anchor case itself.
