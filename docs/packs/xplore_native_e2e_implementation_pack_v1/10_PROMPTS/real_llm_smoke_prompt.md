# Real LLM Smoke Lesson

Generate a Grade 7 Science lesson titled **How Light Helps Plants Make Food**.

Objective:

> Explain that light provides energy plants use to make food and use evidence to distinguish a lit leaf from a covered leaf.

Learners already know:

- plants need water;
- leaves receive sunlight.

Misconception to address:

> Plants take ready-made food from soil.

Keep the lesson within Grade 7 scope. Do not introduce detailed biochemical pathways.

Use four sections:

1. orient the learner;
2. explain the sequence and include a pending figure;
3. compare lit and covered leaf evidence and model reasoning;
4. check understanding with one open-response question and one MCQ.
