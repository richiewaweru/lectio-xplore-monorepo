1 How plants make food
Word equation for photosynthesis
light energy + water +
carbon dioxide
in the presence of
chlorophyll
→
glucose +
oxygen
Chlorophyll captures the light
Chlorophyll absorbs light energy in plant cells, and
photosynthesis stores that energy as chemical energy in
glucose.
Water and carbon dioxide supply the atoms for that glucose, and oxygen leaves the plant as
a gas. The light is not kept as light. It is transferred into the sugar the plant builds, so the
plant holds that energy in a chemical form it can use later.
