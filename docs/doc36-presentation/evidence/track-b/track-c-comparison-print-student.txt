Photosynthesis and respiration
Photosynthesis
Inputs: carbon dioxide, water, and light
energy. Outputs: glucose and oxygen.
Role: stores light energy in glucose.
Cellular respiration
Inputs: glucose and oxygen. Outputs: car-
bon dioxide, water, and released energy.
Role: releases the energy stored in glu-
cose so the cell can use it.
How the pathways connect
Photosynthesis stores energy in glucose, and cellular
respiration releases that energy, so the two pathways
feed each other's inputs and outputs.
Cellular respiration uses glucose and oxygen and gives off carbon dioxide and water.
Photosynthesis uses carbon dioxide and water and gives off glucose and oxygen.
Because each pathway supplies what the other needs, matter cycles between them.
