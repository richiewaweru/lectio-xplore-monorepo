How Light Helps Plants Make Food

Audience: student

Why light matters

Leaves are often positioned where they can receive light. Light is not the plant's food; it supplies the energy the leaf uses to make food from raw materials.

Do not confuse raw materials with food

Soil supplies water and minerals. The plant makes its food rather than taking ready-made food from the soil.

From light to food

A simple sequence:

  1. Light reaches the leaf.
  2. The leaf uses light energy.
  3. The plant makes food from raw materials.

Reasoning from the evidence

Problem: A leaf is partly covered while the rest remains in light. Food-making evidence is later found only in the uncovered part. What conclusion is supported?

  1. Identify what changed: access to light.
  2. Check that both parts belong to the same leaf and share other conditions.
  3. Compare the result: evidence appears in the lit part but not the covered part.
  4. Connect the changed condition to the different result.

Answer: The comparison supports the conclusion that light is required for the leaf to make food.
